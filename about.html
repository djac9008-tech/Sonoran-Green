About Sonoran Green

Small Business. Big Standards.

Our Story

Dalton Jacobs
Owner & Operator

Growing up in a small town in rural Ohio and serving as a Seabee in the Navy taught me what matters most: do quality work and take care of one another. Whether it was my working-class roots or military service, I learned that attention to detail and honest work aren't optional, they're everything.

Now I'm pursuing my graduate degree at Arizona State University, which brought me here to the Phoenix Metro area. Now that I'm here, I want to connect with and serve the local communities around me. That's why Sonoran Green was born.

When you choose Sonoran Green, you're getting military precision paired with good old-fashioned integrity. Knowing I've done right by my neighbors and leaving them satisfied is what it's all about.

What Drives Us

Integrity

Integrity means doing the right thing even when nobody's looking. We bring that same discipline, punctuality, and attention to detail to every window we clean.

Satisfied Customers

You'll get authentic and friendly customer service. We respond promptly, communicate clearly, and treat you and your home with respect. You're not just another job on the schedule, you're a neighbor we're here to serve.

Community Focus

We're not just a window cleaning service, we're your neighbors. Based right here in Gilbert, we're invested in making our local neighborhoods a cleaner, brighter place, one window at a time.

Environmental Responsibility

We believe in protecting Arizona's beautiful environment. That's why we exclusively use EPA-approved, eco-friendly cleaning solutions that are safe for your family, your pets, and our desert ecosystem.
